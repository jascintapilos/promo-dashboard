11.1.1 All promotions offered are limited to one account per member.
11.1.2 Only one account per person, household address, IP address, email address, mobile number, bank account, or shared computer is allowed.
11.1.3 If any member is found to have multiple accounts or engage in collusion, all bonuses, winnings, and funds will be confiscated immediately.

### **11.2 Promotion Claim & Usage**

11.2.1 Promotions can only be claimed after completing a deposit (where applicable) and must be claimed via the Rewards Page.
11.2.2 Once a promotion has been claimed, it cannot be cancelled unless provider maintenance prevents participation.
11.2.3 This promotion cannot be used in conjunction with other promotions unless otherwise stated.

### **11.3 Bonus Validity, Withdrawal & Forfeiture**

11.3.1 Promotion codes are valid for the stated period unless otherwise specified.
11.3.2 Winnings from bonus funds will be removed if the applicable turnover or conditions are not fulfilled.
11.3.3 Members cannot make withdrawals while a promotion is active or if turnover/promotion requirements are not yet met.
11.3.4 Money won using bonus funds will be removed from your account if the prerequisites are not fulfilled.

### **11.4 Valid Turnover & Bet Settlement**

11.4.1 Valid turnover is calculated based on settled bets with winning or losing results.
11.4.2 Tie, draw, push, rejected, invalid bets, and bets with odds below the minimum requirement do not count towards turnover requirements.
11.4.3 Minimal risk wagering, betting on both sides (opposite betting/hedging), and unfair betting patterns do not count towards required turnover.
11.4.4 Any free spin bonus rounds must be fully completed before turnover from those spins is calculated.

11.4.5 Only bets placed under the "Today" category are eligible for Sports promotions, unless otherwise stated.

11.4.6 Bets placed under the "Early" category are strictly excluded from the promotion and will not be counted towards any turnover requirement.

11.4.7 For Sports pre-match promotions, only bets that are both placed and fully settled within 24 hours from the time the bet is placed will be counted towards the turnover requirement. Bets that remain unsettled after the 24-hour period will not be counted.

11.4.8 Bonus is not valid for outright bets with any sports provider. If bonus funds are used to place any outright bets, such bets will be automatically cancelled and forfeited.

11.4.9 Additional Sports Restrictions
  - Bets with odds lower than 0.50 (Malay Odds) or 1.50 (Decimal Odds) will not count towards turnover.
  - Bets resulting in a draw outcome will not count towards turnover.
  - Betting on both sides (opposite betting/hedging) on the same event or market is strictly prohibited and will not count towards turnover.

### **11.5 Game Contribution & Exclusions**

Unless otherwise stated:

11.5.1 Turnover on non-live Table games and non-Slot games will not count towards turnover requirements.
11.5.2 Bets on any type of Blackjack table/game do not contribute to turnover or rebate.
11.5.3 Slot bonuses apply only to slot machine games; turnover on non-slot games does not count.
11.5.4 Sports bonuses apply only to Sports and eSports wagers; Virtual Sports, Mini Games, Lottery, World Events, and Number Games do not count.
