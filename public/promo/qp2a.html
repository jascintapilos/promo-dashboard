How to read this. "Never deposited" is the share of a code's claimers who have no deposit on record — for a free giveaway, that is money handed out with nothing back. The never-deposited % (amber) is the abuse signal; a high leaked RM with a low % just means a big code, not a farm. EXCLUSIVE_KICKSTART_50FC is the clear action — 83% of 2,819 claimers never deposited. The two 2023 TELE codes are already dead.
The honest caveat: cutting every ringgit of leakage only moves bonus cost from 6.77% to 6.25% — still above the 4.5% ceiling. Leakage is a cleanup win; the wider over-spend is a separate, bigger question (the per-pillar effectiveness review).
