Eligible categories clause (clause 4) for Sports-category deposit promos must exclude both Virtual Sports AND Number Games.

EN clause 4: "This promotion is redeemable on Sports game providers only. Turnover will only be calculated for games played under the Sports category, except Virtual Sports and Number Games."

ZH clause 4: "此优惠仅可在体育游戏提供商兑换。流水仅计算在体育类别下进行的游戏，虚拟体育及数字游戏除外。"

Parallel: [[feedback_promo_template_copy_rules]] — LC excludes Blackjack; Sports excludes Virtual Sports and Number Games.

**Why:** operator rule; confirmed 2026-06-17. Number Games is excluded alongside Virtual Sports (confirmed 2026-06-17 after initial omission).
**How to apply:** any time bonus_type=Deposit and categories_only includes SPORT, use the full clause above for clause 4 (not just Virtual Sports alone).
