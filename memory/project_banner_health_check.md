`bin/banner-health-check.mjs` (Opus-built, live-verified 2026-06-11). Probes every live QPRO+QP2 BO for banner-maintenance problems. Read-only — never writes to a BO.

**Homepage composition** (per brand/merchant, the PRIMARY intent — Jascinta 2026-06-11): looks at banners Active AND live-now on user portal (platform_type_id=1, start<=now<=end), grouped per merchant (QP2 by site_id).
- `TOO_FEW_BANNERS` — fewer than `--min` (default 5) live homepage banners. Clean/high-value (10 brands flagged on first run).
- `OUT_OF_ORDER` — banners not arranged in-house→PP→other by position. Provider read from label by keyword: PP_RE (Pragmatic Play/PP) → pp; PROVIDER_OTHER list (Microgaming/FastSpin/Playtech/etc.) → other; else → inhouse. RANK inhouse=0<pp=1<other=2; any inversion (higher-rank cat at lower position) flagged with full seq.
- **CAVEAT (accepted, NOT a bug)**: OUT_OF_ORDER is dominated by evergreen UTILITY banners parked at position 99 (Referral Program, Unlimited Cash Rebate, Fraud Announcement) classed as in-house and sitting below provider banners. Jascinta was offered a pos>=90 / utility-label exclusion 2026-06-11 and chose to LEAVE IT AS-IS — do not "fix" without asking. TOO_FEW is the clean/primary signal.

**Four date/activation flags** (per banner):
- `ALREADY_EXPIRED` — Active banner whose end_datetime is past, expired within `--lookback` days (default 30). Older stale-active banners counted as backlog, not listed.
- `EXPIRING_SOON` — Active banner ending within `--expiring` days (default 3).
- `NOT_ACTIVATED` — Inactive/draft banner whose window is open NOW and scheduled within `--lookback` days (excludes evergreen 2020→2036 parked drafts).
- `ENDS_BEFORE_CAMPAIGN` — Active banner ending >1 day before its campaign's End Date (Banner Schedule sheet col L, current-month tab). **Heuristic**: matches banner→campaign by brand + overlapping date window (sheet has no promo code), so the named campaign is a best guess for a human to confirm.

**Data sources**: `getAllBanners(site,{status})` (api-client) for active+inactive rows; banner `link` = `/promotion?code=<CODE>`, `end_datetime` is ISO UTC. QP2 banners carry `site_id` (1=IBC22,2=KING333,3=ACE66,4=SPADE66) and the merchant_id filter is IGNORED → fetch once, group by site_id. ENDS_BEFORE_CAMPAIGN reads the Banner Schedule sheet (`1vpyjhqiKzcn2XHovcN2tEa4UkzUMqTmFsUZ59m-n8_E`) current-month tab via google-auth; col K/L dates are `D-Mon-YYYY` parsed at GMT+8 day boundaries; `lookupBrand` maps col I → {siteId, merchantName}.

**Flags**: `--site=qpro1` · `--expiring=N` · `--lookback=N` · `--no-sheet` (skip x-ref + Google auth) · `--include-canary` (qpro11/qpro13, skipped by default) · `--json` · `--dashboard` (write-back, see below) · `--slack --slack-channel=C...` (opt-in; refuses without a channel; loads token from slack-token.local.json).
