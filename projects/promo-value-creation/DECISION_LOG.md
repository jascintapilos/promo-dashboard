## Decision rules

- Record the final decision, owner, date, and rationale.
- Link supporting evidence where available (see `outputs/pvc-csir-probe/` JSON files).
- A rejected proposal remains in the log with its reason.
- Reopen a decision only when new evidence materially changes the position.
- "Evidence-ready" status means numeric evidence attached; HOD can approve or adjust without further data collection.
